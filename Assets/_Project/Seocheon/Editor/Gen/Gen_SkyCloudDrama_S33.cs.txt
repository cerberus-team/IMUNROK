S33 Sky Cloud Drama — execution record

Goal
- Make the sky the dominant visual element without changing Terrain, flowers,
  planet placement, PlanetDirection, skybox cubemap, or directional-light rotation.

Source
- Assets/RainSnowCloudEffect/Prefabs/PF_CloudLayer.prefab
- Assets/RainSnowCloudEffect/Materials/M_CloudLayer_URP.mat

Owned output
- _SkyCloudDrama
  - Cloud_Crown
  - Cloud_SouthLeft
  - Cloud_SouthRight
  - Cloud_NorthCrown
- Assets/_Project/Seocheon/Art/VFX/S33/M_S33_CloudDrama.mat

Method
- Four deterministic world-space particle layers.
- Fixed random seeds 3301..3304.
- Large lavender / blue / peach forms, slow speed, no colliders, no shadows.
- P1 keeps an opening around the planet while cloud banks frame the upper sky.
- BACK receives a separate north crown instead of reusing the south silhouette.

Immutable
- Terrain heightmap / alphamap / detail layers and TerrainLayers.
- Existing flower roots and user-painted details.
- M_Flowerfield_Sky, CM_Flowerfield_Sky_v14, Aurora, stars.
- Planet, satellite, PlanetDirection and Directional Light rotation.
- Willow and camera baseline.

Safety
- Scene and M_Flowerfield_Sky are copied to timestamped _bk assets first.
- No AssetDatabase.SaveAssets().
- SaveAssetIfDirty is used only for the new S33 material.
- The test root is isolated and removable as one object.
