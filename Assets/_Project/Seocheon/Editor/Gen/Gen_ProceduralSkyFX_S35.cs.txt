S35 - Unity built-in Particle System clouds and aurora

Intent
- Keep the approved S33 lavender/rose/blue palette.
- Remove the painted cloud silhouette by using the S34 high-mip colour-only sky material.
- Do not use PF_CloudLayer, Clouds_Cubemap, LUMINATE, VFX Graph, or imported cloud prefabs.
- Build both effects from Unity's built-in Particle System.

Cloud source
- Assets/Eric VFX Studio/Resource/Textures/FX_smoke_02.png
- Four deterministic, world-space banks of layered square billboards.
- Large overlapping puffs, low alpha, very slow movement, no colliders/shadows.

Aurora source
- Assets/Eric VFX Studio/Resource/Textures/Noise_02.png
- Custom Seocheon/AuroraParticle additive shader.
- Three deterministic banks of tall narrow billboard curtains.
- Magenta/cyan colour range, low alpha, slow drift, kept away from the planet opening.

Hierarchy
_ProceduralSkyFX
  _Clouds
  _Aurora

Safety
- Back up scene before mutation.
- Do not touch terrain, alphamap, details, flowers, fog, planet, satellite, Willow or light rotation.
- Keep all new effects toggleable through one root.
- Save only after visual inspection; SaveAssetIfDirty(target) only.
